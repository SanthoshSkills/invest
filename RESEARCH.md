## 3. Stock Selection Rules
- **Liquidity Minimum:** Average Daily Traded Value (ADTV) > ₹30 Crore (Bull) to > ₹75 Crore (Bear).
- **Quality Filters:** 3-year average ROCE/ROE > 16%, OCF/EBITDA > 70%, Debt-to-Equity < 0.7x (non-financials).
- **Valuation Guardrail:** PEG ratio < 1.75x or current P/E ≤ 5-year historical median P/E.

## 4. Sector & Industry Qualitative Filter

### Priority Tailwinds (Screening Eligibility)
- **Power T&D & Infrastructure:** Transformer & grid capex backlogs (Revenue visibility > 3 years).
- **Pharma & CDMO:** Global supply chain diversification ("China+1") and steady domestic chronic formulations.
- **Financial Services (Secured):** Corporate-facing lenders and wealth managers supported by domestic retail savings.

### Critical Headwinds (Screening Disqualifiers)
- **Unsecured Credit NBFCs & Microfinance:** Rising asset quality pressure and RBI regulatory restrictions.
- **Overvalued Late-Cycle Cyclicals:** Capital goods and consumer discretionary stocks trading at >2.5x PEG or >50x P/E with decelerating quarterly earnings.
- **Mass FMCG:** Volume compression and retail channel margin erosion.
